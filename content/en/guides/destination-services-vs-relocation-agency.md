Moving to Switzerland as a family is a significant step, and many families seek support to make the transition smoother. The two main options are destination services and relocation agencies. Understanding the difference helps you choose the right support for your move.

**Key facts (as of October 2026)**

| Requirement                | Rule                                                                                                 |
|---------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Health insurance           | Compulsory within 3 months of commune registration ([art. 7 KVV](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1995/3867_3867_3867/de#art_7)) |
| Commune registration       | Must be completed within 14 days of entry for stays over 3 months ([SEM Weisungen AIG, art. 10 VZAE](https://www.sem.admin.ch/dam/sem/de/data/rechtsgrundlagen/weisungen/auslaender/weisungen-aug-d.pdf)) |
| Driving licence exchange   | Required after 12 months' residence ([art. 42 para. 3bis VZV](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1976/2423_2423_2423/de#art_42)) |
| Importing goods            | Household goods and vehicles are duty-free if used for at least 6 months and imported as relocation goods ([BAZG – Umzug](https://www.bazg.admin.ch/de/umzug-uebersiedlungsgut)) |
| Schooling                  | International schools are available in major Swiss cities |

## What are destination services in Switzerland?

Destination services in Switzerland are practical, on-the-ground support for families arriving in a new country. These services focus on the essential tasks that help you settle in smoothly. Typical destination services include:

- Accompanying you to register with the local commune (the Swiss resident registration office)
- Guidance on health insurance enrolment, which is compulsory within 3 months of registering with the commune
- Support with opening bank accounts and setting up utilities
- Help with finding local services, such as doctors, childcare and public transport
- Advice on exchanging your driving licence (required after 12 months’ residence)
- Assistance with school search and enrolment, including international and local options

Destination service providers are often local experts who know the area well. Their role is to make the first weeks and months in Switzerland less stressful, ensuring you meet all legal requirements and feel at home in your new surroundings.

## How does a relocation agency support a family move?

A relocation agency in Switzerland offers a broader range of support, often starting before you arrive. Agencies typically coordinate the entire move, including:

- Pre-move orientation and needs assessment
- Home search and lease negotiation
- School search and application support
- Arranging temporary accommodation
- Managing the logistics of moving household goods
- Overseeing administrative steps such as residence permits and insurance
- Providing settling-in support (sometimes through destination service partners)

Relocation agencies act as project managers for your move. They coordinate with local providers, landlords, schools and authorities, aiming to reduce the burden on your family. For families with complex needs or those moving from outside Europe, a relocation agency can be especially helpful in navigating Swiss procedures and expectations.

For a detailed look at what a relocation partner does for a family, see [Relocation services in Switzerland: what a relocation partner does for a family](/en/guides/relocation-services-switzerland/).

## Destination services vs relocation agency: what is the difference?

While both destination services and relocation agencies help families settle in Switzerland, their focus and scope differ. Here’s a comparison:

| Feature         | Destination services                          | Relocation agency                                 |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| Main focus      | Practical settling-in support after arrival   | End-to-end move coordination (before and after)    |
| Typical tasks   | Registration, insurance, daily logistics, school search | Home search, permits, move logistics, school search, settling-in |
| Timing          | Usually starts on arrival                     | Begins before the move, continues after arrival    |
| Who provides it | Local specialists                             | Agency staff (may subcontract destination services)|
| Ongoing support | Sometimes, for settling-in                    | Often, including ongoing advice and troubleshooting|

Some families use both: a relocation agency for the overall move, and destination services for hands-on support once in Switzerland. Others may only need destination services if they have already found a home and handled the move themselves.

## Which support is best for settling in Switzerland with children?

For families with children, settling in Switzerland involves more than paperwork. School search and enrolment, understanding local education options, and arranging childcare are often top priorities. Both destination services and relocation agencies can help, but the best choice depends on your needs:

- **Destination services** are ideal if you want hands-on help with school visits, registration and daily logistics. Local specialists often have direct experience with international and local schools.
- **Relocation agencies** can coordinate the school search as part of a wider move, including liaising with schools and arranging interviews. This is helpful if you are moving from abroad and need support before arrival.

For an overview of international school options, see [International schools in Switzerland: a regional guide](/en/guides/international-schools-switzerland/).

## Do you need both destination services and a relocation agency?

Not every family needs both. If you are comfortable managing the move and finding a home, destination services may be enough to help you settle in. If you prefer a single point of contact for the entire process, a relocation agency can coordinate everything, sometimes working with destination service partners for the practical tasks.

Some families start with a relocation agency for the move and continue with destination services for ongoing support. Others may choose one or the other, depending on their confidence, language skills and the complexity of their move.

## What are the first steps after arriving in Switzerland?

The first weeks in Switzerland involve several legal and practical steps. Key tasks include:

- **Registering with the commune** within 14 days of arrival if you plan to stay over 3 months ([SEM Weisungen AIG, art. 10 VZAE](https://www.sem.admin.ch/dam/sem/de/data/rechtsgrundlagen/weisungen/auslaender/weisungen-aug-d.pdf))
- **Arranging compulsory health insurance** within 3 months of registration ([art. 7 KVV](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1995/3867_3867_3867/de#art_7))
- **Enrolling children in school** (public or international)
- **Opening a bank account** and setting up utilities
- **Exchanging your driving licence** within 12 months ([art. 42 para. 3bis VZV](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1976/2423_2423_2423/de#art_42))
- **Importing household goods and vehicles** duty-free if used for at least 6 months ([BAZG – Umzug](https://www.bazg.admin.ch/de/umzug-uebersiedlungsgut))

A clear checklist can help you keep track. See [Your first 90 days in Switzerland: a new-resident checklist](/en/guides/first-90-days-checklist/).

## How ongoing support helps families adjust

Settling in does not end after the first few weeks. Ongoing support can make a real difference, especially for families adjusting to a new language, school system and way of life. Ongoing services may include:

- Help with local authorities, renewals and paperwork
- Guidance on finding clubs, activities and social networks
- Support with everyday challenges, such as understanding local customs or finding healthcare providers
- Assistance with changes in family circumstances, such as a new baby or a move within Switzerland

Some relocation agencies and destination service providers offer ongoing support packages. For families who want reassurance and a local contact, this can be valuable. For more, see [After the move: ongoing wealth and household support](/en/guides/ongoing-support-after-moving/).
