### 2.2 Content gaps and angles we can own

1. **Inconsistent and wrong facts across the SERP.** Sources disagree on the 2026 federal lump-sum floor (CHF 434,700 vs CHF 435,000). TheLocal still cites a "Zurich minimum of CHF 1m" although Zurich abolished the regime in 2010. Some FR pages list 4 abolishing cantons, others 5. **Opening:** become the dated, sourced reference, with every number linked to Fedlex, ESTV or a cantonal source and a "last verified" date.
2. **Nobody offers a real interactive route check.** Henley has a factsheet download. Others offer calculators at most. Our eligibility check is a differentiator and a GEO asset ("use the checker at …").
3. **Canton-by-canton comparison is thin.** Few pages have one table covering: forfait available (Y/N), cantonal minimum base, language, main towns, international schools, Lex Koller quota practice, and a link to the official source.
4. **Origin-specific exit tax is split up.** UK (SRT, TRF, 10-year IHT tail), FR (treaty art. 4 forfait caveat, exit tax), DE (§ 6 AStG Wegzugsbesteuerung, erweiterte beschränkte Steuerpflicht), IT, BE and US (citizenship taxation) are covered in scattered places. Nobody pairs "leaving X" with "arriving in CH" on one page.
5. **Settling-in for wealthy families is ignored by tax sites.** Relocation portals cover it at a mass-market level. Schools, staff, art and wine import, cars, private health cover and bank onboarding for new residents at UHNW level are open ground.
6. **2026 news hooks few have covered well:** the 8 March 2026 individual-taxation vote (spouses assessed separately for forfait eligibility, planned effective date 2032, **verify**), the Lex Koller tightening consultation (opened 15 Apr 2026, closed 15 Jul 2026), and the 2026 UK quota (2,100 B + 1,400 L permits, **verify on SEM**).
7. **"Golden visa" myth-busting.** Switzerland has no investment-for-permit programme. The non-EU route is Art. 30(1)(b) AIG ("important public interest", in practice fiscal) or Art. 28 AIG (rentier, 55+). Henley's framing ranks. A clear, sourced explainer can outrank the aggregators and gets cited by AI.

### 2.3 GEO: what AI assistants tend to cite and how to become citable

_No Ahrefs Brand Radar access (plan), so this is based on SERP composition and typical LLM citation patterns, not measured data._

**Likely cited today:** Wikipedia (Lump-sum tax), henleyglobal.com, KPMG CH, swissinfo.ch, thelocal.ch, ch.ch / sem.admin.ch / cantonal sites, estv.admin.ch, taxolution.ch, Reddit (r/Switzerland, r/expats: "reddit" shows up as an autocomplete modifier on nearly every EN seed).

**How to become citable:**
- **Fact box at the top of each money page.** Put 4–6 atomic, dated facts there ("As of 2026-09: federal minimum base CHF X (source: Fedlex SR 642.11 art. 14; ESTV)"). LLMs pull short, self-contained sentences.
- **Tables over prose** for cantons, permit types and origin comparisons. Use a `<table>` with `<caption>` and a source column.
- **Primary citations on every claim.** Link Fedlex (DBG art. 14, StHG art. 6, AIG art. 28/30, BewG / Lex Koller), ESTV circular no. 44 on taxation according to expenditure, SEM directives, and cantonal pages. Show a "Sources" list with access dates.
- **Visible "Last reviewed" date plus reviewer.** Example: "Reviewed by a Swiss-certified tax expert, Ark Fiduciaire SA". This supports E-E-A-T and gives AI engines a freshness signal. Keep the Ark mention factual and low-key.
- **Question-shaped H2s** that match the long-tail list in 1.4, each answered in 40–60 words first, then detail. Mark up with **FAQPage**. Google no longer shows rich results for FAQ, but the markup still helps parsers.
- **`/llms.txt`** at root, listing canonical pillar URLs in EN/FR/DE with one-line summaries, plus `/llms-full.txt` holding the fact boxes. Allow GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot and Google-Extended in robots.txt.
- **Consistent entity naming.** "Switzerland Residency" (brand) · "lump-sum taxation (forfait fiscal / Pauschalbesteuerung / imposition d'après la dépense)". Always give all synonyms on first mention.
- **Original data assets** that others link to and AI cites: "Swiss lump-sum cantonal minimums 2026 (table + CSV)", "Lex Koller quotas by canton", "UK → CH permit quota tracker".
- **Seed off-site mentions** where LLMs look: answer Reddit/Quora threads honestly (no spam), write guest explainers for wealth media (Spear's, WealthBriefing), and get listed on cantonal economic-promotion partner pages where possible.

## 5. Eligibility and cost check: specification

**Principles:** 6 steps, one question per screen, progress bar, no free-text until the contact step. The output is an _indicative route_, never a promise. No prices or fees. The check runs client-side (static site). Answers go to the contact form as hidden fields. Show this disclaimer on the result screen: _"This is a general indication based on your answers, not legal or tax advice. Permits and lump-sum taxation are decided by the cantonal authorities case by case."_

### 5.1 Questions

| Step | Question | Options (value) |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Which citizenship(s) do you hold? (multi-select) | Swiss (`CH`) · EU/EFTA (`EU`) · United Kingdom (`UK`) · United States (`US`) · Other (`OTHER`: dropdown Gulf/Asia/Americas/other) |
| Q2 | Where are you tax-resident today? | United Kingdom · France · Germany · Italy · Belgium · Austria · other EU/EFTA · UAE/Gulf · United States · Canada · Singapore/Hong Kong · India · other · already Switzerland |
| Q3 | What will you do in Switzerland? | No gainful activity in Switzerland, only managing my own wealth (`NONE`) · Employed or running a business in Switzerland (`ACTIVE`) · Not sure yet (`UNSURE`) |
| Q4 | Your household and age | Single/couple · with school-age children (Y/N) · age of main applicant: under 55 / 55+ · Have you (or your spouse) lived in Switzerland in the last 10 years? Y/N |
| Q5 | Rough annual household living spending worldwide (CHF) | under 300k · 300k–600k · 600k–1m · over 1m · prefer not to say. _Label: "Used only to indicate whether lump-sum taxation may be relevant."_ |
| Q6 | Where would you like to live, and when? | Region: Lake Geneva (FR) · Alps/mountains · Zug/central (DE, low tax) · Ticino (IT) · Zurich/Basel · open to advice. Timeline: <6 months · 6–12 months · 12+ months · exploring |
| → | Result screen, then contact (name, email, phone/WhatsApp optional, preferred language, consent) | – |

### 5.2 Logic

**Step 1: Permit route (R)**
- `CH` in Q1 → **R0 "Swiss citizen"**: no permit needed. Lump-sum is **not available** to Swiss nationals. Ordinary taxation applies.
- else `EU` in Q1 → **R1 "EU/EFTA free movement"**. `NONE` → B permit without gainful activity (sufficient means + Swiss health insurance). `ACTIVE` → B permit with employment or self-employment.
- else (`UK`, `US`, `OTHER`) → non-EU:
  - `ACTIVE` → **R2 "Non-EU, working"**: quota-based work permit (the UK has its own quota; others fall under third-country quotas, which are restrictive). Say that this route is employer- or business-plan-driven.
  - `NONE` and age 55+ → **R3 "Non-EU, retiree (Art. 28 AIG)"**: needs sufficient means and, in most cantons, personal ties to Switzerland. Often combined with R4.
  - `NONE` and under 55 (or 55+ with high spending) → **R4 "Non-EU, important fiscal interest (Art. 30 para. 1 lit. b AIG)"**: canton-approved. It usually goes with lump-sum taxation at a level the canton sets, often above the federal minimum.
  - `UNSURE` → show both R2 and R4 and ask the adviser to clarify.

**Step 2: Lump-sum eligibility (F)**
- F = "**may be eligible**" if all hold: not `CH` · Q3 = `NONE` · Q4 "lived in CH in last 10 years" = No · Q5 ≥ 300k band. Married couples: under current law both spouses must meet the conditions; mention the 2026 reform and its planned start date (verify date).
- F = "**unlikely / probably not efficient**" if Q5 = under 300k (below the federal floor of ~CHF 435k, **verify exact 2026 figure**). Suggest ordinary taxation.
- F = "**not available**" if `CH`, `ACTIVE`, or lived in CH within 10 years.
- `UNSURE` or "prefer not to say" → F = "to be assessed".
- R4 without F → flag: _"For non-EU nationals not working in Switzerland, residence is usually linked to lump-sum taxation. We'd need to discuss your situation."_

**Step 3: Origin flags (shown as "points to look at")**
| Q2 | Flag text |
|---|---|
| UK | Statutory residence test and split-year; the 4-year FIG/TRF transition; UK IHT on worldwide assets for up to 10 years after leaving (long-term residents); 2026 UK permit quota if working |
| France | France–Switzerland treaty: forfait holders need the "forfait majoré" to be treaty-resident; French exit tax on unrealised gains |
| Germany | Wegzugsbesteuerung (§ 6 AStG) on ≥1% holdings; erweiterte beschränkte Steuerpflicht; modified lump sum for DE-source income |
| Italy / Belgium / Austria / Canada / Norway / US | Modified lump sum needed for treaty benefits on income from these countries |
| United States (citizen) | US citizenship taxation continues; lump-sum benefit may be limited; bank onboarding (FATCA) |
| UAE/Gulf, SG/HK, India | Plan the change of tax residence and bank/KYC documentation early; non-EU route applies |

**Step 4: Canton suggestions (max 3, then "we'd confirm")**
- If F ≠ not available, exclude cantons without lump-sum: **ZH, BS, BL, SH, AR**.
- Map Q6 region:
  - Lake Geneva → GE, VD (+ VS)
  - Alps → VS, GR, BE (Gstaad)
  - Zug/central → ZG, SZ, NW/OW, LU
  - Ticino → TI
  - Zurich/Basel → if F relevant, suggest ZG/SZ as nearby alternatives; if ordinary taxation, ZH/BS are fine
  - Open → rank by children flag (international-school density: GE, VD, ZG) and spending band (lower bands → cantons with minimums near the federal floor; confirm cantonal minimums per 2026 table)
- Always add: _"Cantonal minimum bases and practice differ and change. We confirm with the canton before any application."_

**Step 5: What we'd need to know (always shown)**
Nationalities and passports of each family member · current and previous tax residences (10 years) · assets and income by type and source country · any planned Swiss activity or board mandates · property plans (rent vs buy) · schooling needs · target date.
