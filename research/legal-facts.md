# switzerlandresidency.ch — legal & tax fact base

Research date: **2026-09-30**. Every figure carries a source and "(v. 2026-09-30)" = verified that day on the cited page/document. **UNVERIFIED** = only secondary sources found, or not confirmed on an official source. Do not publish UNVERIFIED items as fact without re-checking.

Conventions: CHF amounts. "Federal" = direct federal tax (DBG/LIFD). "Cantonal" = cantonal + communal tax (StHG/LHID + cantonal law). Cantonal fact sheets ("Kantonsblätter", ESTV, **Stand Februar 2026**) are at `https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/kb/<canton>-<lang>.pdf`, abbreviated **[KB-xx]** below.

## 1. Lump-sum taxation (Besteuerung nach dem Aufwand / imposition d'après la dépense / forfait fiscal)

### 1.1 Legal basis
- Federal: **art. 14 DBG/LIFD** (SR 642.11), current version in force since 1 Jan 2016. [Fedlex art. 14 DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de#art_14) (v. 2026-09-30)
- Cantonal framework: **art. 6 StHG/LHID** (SR 642.14), in force since 1 Jan 2014; cantons *may* grant it ("Der Kanton kann…"). [Fedlex art. 6 StHG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1256_1256_1256/de#art_6) (v. 2026-09-30)
- Federal ordinance: Verordnung vom 20. Feb. 2013 über die Besteuerung nach dem Aufwand (SR 642.123), in force 1 Jan 2016. [ESTV Circular 44](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/pl0PXAcAAxJq/dbst-ks-2018-1-044-d-de.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
- Administrative guidance: **ESTV Circular (Kreisschreiben) No. 44 of 24 July 2018**. Old-law taxpayers kept the pre-2016 rules until 31 Dec 2020; from 1 Jan 2021 the new art. 14 DBG applies to everyone. [ESTV KS 44](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/pl0PXAcAAxJq/dbst-ks-2018-1-044-d-de.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)

### 1.2 Eligibility (art. 14 para. 1–2 DBG; KS 44 §2)
- Not Swiss nationality. Swiss dual nationals count as Swiss and **are excluded**. Acquiring Swiss citizenship ends the regime for the whole tax period of naturalisation. [KS 44 §2.2](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/pl0PXAcAAxJq/dbst-ks-2018-1-044-d-de.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
- Unlimited tax liability in Switzerland **for the first time or after at least 10 years' absence**. People who were taxed on a lump sum before and come back may use it again regardless of the 10-year rule. [art. 14 DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de#art_14); [KS 44 §2.3](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/pl0PXAcAAxJq/dbst-ks-2018-1-044-d-de.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
- **No gainful activity in Switzerland** (any main or side occupation carried out in CH, even if paid abroad). KS 44 names artists, scientists, inventors, athletes and board members working in CH. Retired diplomats/international civil servants who stay on do not qualify. [KS 44 §2.3](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/pl0PXAcAAxJq/dbst-ks-2018-1-044-d-de.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
  - Cantonal nuance (Uri): an unpaid board seat is allowed if it only serves to manage your own assets or is honorary. [UR Merkblatt 1.1.2026](https://www.ur.ch/_docn/439772/14_Merkblatt_Aufwandbesteuerung_01.01.2026_1.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
- **Both spouses** must meet all conditions. If one spouse is or becomes Swiss, or works in CH, both lose the regime for the whole period. [art. 14 para. 2 DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de#art_14); [KS 44 §2.4](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/pl0PXAcAAxJq/dbst-ks-2018-1-044-d-de.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
- Once you give it up for ordinary taxation, you generally cannot return to the lump sum. [KS 44 §2.5](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/pl0PXAcAAxJq/dbst-ks-2018-1-044-d-de.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)

### 1.3 Federal tax base 2026
- **CHF 435,000** minimum base for tax year **2026** (CHF 434,700 in 2025), indexed by the EFD ordinance of 10 Sep 2025 on cold progression (AS 2025 579). [ESTV Rundschreiben 2-215-D-2025 (11 Sep 2025)](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/vFK3ntWLQ4s4/2-215-D-2025-d.pdf); [art. 14 para. 3 lit. a DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de#art_14) (v. 2026-09-30)
- The base is the **worldwide annual living costs** of the taxpayer and dependants (in CH and abroad), but at least the **highest** of: (a) CHF 435,000; (b) **7× annual rent or rental value** of the home (if own household); (c) 3× the annual board-and-lodging price (hotel/pension); (d) the control calculation (see 1.4). [art. 14 para. 3 DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de#art_14) (v. 2026-09-30)
- Living costs include taxes and social-security contributions, staff, schooling abroad, travel, horses, cars, boats, aircraft, etc. With several Swiss homes, the highest rent/rental value is used for the 7× test. No deductions allowed from the base. [KS 44 §3.2–3.3](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/pl0PXAcAAxJq/dbst-ks-2018-1-044-d-de.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
  - Because taxes count as living costs, cantons compute a gross-up (iteration) — worked examples: [GE third-country example PDF](https://www.ge.ch/document/28085/telecharger); [GE EU/EFTA example PDF](https://www.ge.ch/document/28084/telecharger) (v. 2026-09-30)
- Ordinary federal tariff applies; no social deductions. [art. 14 para. 4 DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de#art_14); [KS 44 §4](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/pl0PXAcAAxJq/dbst-ks-2018-1-044-d-de.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
- Pending changes to the DBG are listed for 2027, 2028 and 2029 (the 2029 one includes the abolition of imputed rental value, see §1.9). Whether and how the "7× rental value" test changes after 2029 is **UNVERIFIED**. [Fedlex/OpenCaseLaw pending-changes flag on art. 14 DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de#art_14)

### 1.4 Control calculation (art. 14 para. 3 lit. d DBG; art. 6 para. 6 StHG)
- *(Wording corrected 2026-09-30: under current art. 14 para. 3 lit. d DBG the gross total is a floor of the **base**, not a separate minimum tax; the practical effect is the same.)* The base must be at least the **gross** total of: Swiss real estate and its income; movable property in CH and its income; capital invested in CH (incl. mortgage-secured loans) and its income; copyrights/patents used in CH; **Swiss-source pensions**; and **foreign income for which DTA relief is claimed**. [art. 14 DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de#art_14); [art. 6 StHG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1256_1256_1256/de#art_6) (v. 2026-09-30)
- Swiss source = debtor domiciled in CH (bonds) or company seated in CH (shares); custody place and currency do not matter. Only property maintenance costs and ordinary asset-management costs are deductible in the control calculation. [KS 44 §3.3.4](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/pl0PXAcAAxJq/dbst-ks-2018-1-044-d-de.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
- Cantonal level: the control calculation also includes the gross **wealth** items (Swiss real estate, Swiss movables, capital invested in CH). [art. 6 para. 6 StHG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1256_1256_1256/de#art_6) (v. 2026-09-30)

### 1.5 Modified lump sum and DTA specifics
- Lump-sum taxpayers **can** claim Swiss DTA relief (e.g. reduced foreign withholding tax), but must declare it; relief-claimed foreign income enters the control calculation. [KS 44 §5.1](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/pl0PXAcAAxJq/dbst-ks-2018-1-044-d-de.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
- **Modified lump sum** — under the DTAs with **Belgium, Germany, Italy, Norway, Canada, Austria and the USA**, treaty benefits are only granted if **all** income from that state is taxed in CH as under ordinary taxation. All income from that state is then added to the base and taxed at the rate for total income. If full data is not provided, the maximum rate applies. [KS 44 §5.2](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/pl0PXAcAAxJq/dbst-ks-2018-1-044-d-de.pdf); [art. 14 para. 5 DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de#art_14) (v. 2026-09-30)
- KS 44 §3.3.4 also mentions **France** among treaties with "special rules" (in addition to the 7 above). [KS 44](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/pl0PXAcAAxJq/dbst-ks-2018-1-044-d-de.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30). Exact French rule (residence status of lump-sum taxpayers under the CH-FR DTA) — **UNVERIFIED**.
- The **UK is not** on the ESTV list of treaties requiring the modified lump sum. [KS 44](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/pl0PXAcAAxJq/dbst-ks-2018-1-044-d-de.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30). Any UK-specific treaty rule for lump-sum taxpayers — **UNVERIFIED**.
- Lump-sum taxpayers generally have **no right to the flat-rate foreign tax credit** (pauschale Steueranrechnung), except under the 7 treaties above. [KS 44 §5.2](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/pl0PXAcAAxJq/dbst-ks-2018-1-044-d-de.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
- Uri's application checklist asks explicitly whether you claim DTA relief from "USA, Germany, Belgium, Norway, Italy, Canada or Austria". [UR Merkblatt](https://www.ur.ch/_docn/439772/14_Merkblatt_Aufwandbesteuerung_01.01.2026_1.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)

### 1.6 Cantons that abolished it
- **Zurich** (vote 8 Feb 2009, effective 1 Jan 2010), **Schaffhausen**, **Appenzell Ausserrhoden**, **Basel-Landschaft**, **Basel-Stadt**. [EFD – Lump-sum taxation](https://www.efd.admin.ch/en/lump-sum-taxation) (v. 2026-09-30)
  - Cross-check: the ESTV fact sheets (Feb 2026) for ZH, SH, AR and BS contain no lump-sum section. [KB-zh](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/kb/zh-de.pdf), [KB-sh](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/kb/sh-de.pdf), [KB-ar](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/kb/ar-de.pdf), [KB-bs](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/kb/bs-de.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
  - **BL** still has § 10bis StG, but the right only runs "until the end of the current tax period" (i.e. the arrival year only). [KB-bl](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/kb/bl-de.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)

- Thurgau, St. Gallen, Lucerne and Bern kept it but tightened the rules. [EFD](https://www.efd.admin.ch/en/lump-sum-taxation) (v. 2026-09-30)
- **Statistics:** end 2018: **4,557** lump-sum taxpayers paid **CHF 821 m**; fewer than 0.1% of taxpayers. [EFD](https://www.efd.admin.ch/en/lump-sum-taxation) (v. 2026-09-30). Ticino: 767 cases and CHF 183.5 m in total tax in 2022. [TI communiqué 4 May 2023](https://www4.ti.ch/tich/area-media/comunicati/dettaglio-comunicato?NEWS_ID=221229) (v. 2026-09-30). ### 1.8 Application procedure (examples)
- **Geneva:** written request to the cantonal tax administration on arrival, together with the migration procedure: a letter explaining your situation and confirming no gainful activity in CH, a **proposed lump-sum amount**, a copy of the lease or property valuation, and a lifestyle form ("train de vie"; FR/EN forms available). [ge.ch – comment bénéficier](https://www.ge.ch/imposition-apres-depense/comment-beneficier-imposition-apres-depense) (v. 2026-09-30)
- **Uri:** application to the Amt für Steuern with personal situation and **worldwide income and wealth**, a detailed checklist and a living-cost budget. Every year you must file the special lump-sum return and document **worldwide living costs**. [UR Merkblatt](https://www.ur.ch/_docn/439772/14_Merkblatt_Aufwandbesteuerung_01.01.2026_1.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
- Every year the tax authority must check that the conditions are still met, and the taxpayer must report changes without being asked. [KS 44 §5.1](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/pl0PXAcAAxJq/dbst-ks-2018-1-044-d-de.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)

## 2. Residence permits

### 2.1 EU/EFTA nationals without gainful activity (AFMP/FZA)
- Retirees, people of independent means ("Privatiers") and other non-employed people may live in CH with their family if they have **sufficient financial means** and **comprehensive health and accident insurance** (art. 24 para. 1 Annex I FZA). No quotas. The restrictive AIG/VZAE conditions (art. 23–25 VZAE) do not apply. [SEM Weisungen VFP (Jan 2026) §6.2](https://www.sem.admin.ch/dam/sem/de/data/rechtsgrundlagen/weisungen/fza/weisungen-fza-d.pdf.download.pdf/weisungen-fza-d.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
- "Sufficient" = above the level at which a Swiss person could claim social assistance (SKOS guidelines, art. 16 VFP). For **retirees**, means must exceed the threshold for supplementary benefits (Ergänzungsleistungen, ELG). [SEM Weisungen VFP §6.2.3](https://www.sem.admin.ch/dam/sem/de/data/rechtsgrundlagen/weisungen/fza/weisungen-fza-d.pdf.download.pdf/weisungen-fza-d.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
- The B EU/EFTA permit for non-employed people is normally valid **5 years**. It can be withdrawn if means or insurance fall away. [SEM Weisungen VFP](https://www.sem.admin.ch/dam/sem/de/data/rechtsgrundlagen/weisungen/fza/weisungen-fza-d.pdf.download.pdf/weisungen-fza-d.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
- **B → C:** C permit after **5 years** for nationals covered by settlement agreements. For **BG, EE, HR, LV, LT, MT, PL, RO, SK, SI, CZ, HU, CY** there is no treaty right: C is normally granted after 10 years. The C EU/EFTA card is checked every 5 years. [SEM Weisungen VFP](https://www.sem.admin.ch/dam/sem/de/data/rechtsgrundlagen/weisungen/fza/weisungen-fza-d.pdf.download.pdf/weisungen-fza-d.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
- The lump sum can be combined with an EU permit. [UR Merkblatt](https://www.ur.ch/_docn/439772/14_Merkblatt_Aufwandbesteuerung_01.01.2026_1.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)

### 2.2 UK nationals after Brexit
- From **1 Jan 2021**, UK nationals not covered by the Citizens' Rights Agreement are subject to the ordinary **AIG (third-country) rules**. People admitted before that keep acquired rights. [SEM Weisungen AIG §5.3](https://www.sem.admin.ch/dam/sem/de/data/rechtsgrundlagen/weisungen/auslaender/weisungen-aug-d.pdf) (Stand 15 Jun 2026; v. 2026-09-30)
- The SEM approval procedure treats UK nationals as non-EU/EFTA unless stated otherwise. [SEM Weisungen AIG §1.3.1](https://www.sem.admin.ch/dam/sem/de/data/rechtsgrundlagen/weisungen/auslaender/weisungen-aug-d.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)

### 2.3 Non-EU nationals: retirees (art. 28 AIG, art. 25 VZAE)
Cumulative conditions: [SEM Weisungen AIG §5.3](https://www.sem.admin.ch/dam/sem/de/data/rechtsgrundlagen/weisungen/auslaender/weisungen-aug-d.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
- **Minimum age 55**
- **Special personal ties to Switzerland.** Examples: long or repeated earlier stays (e.g. regular holidays), close relatives in CH, Swiss ancestry. **Owning property or having business ties is not enough.** Case law requires ties to Switzerland itself, not only to relatives here.
- **Sufficient financial means**: above the supplementary-benefits level, and reliably available for life.
- **No gainful activity in CH or abroad** (except managing your own assets). You must prove you have retired and commit not to work.
- The centre of your life must be in CH, or renewal is refused. Spouse and minor children may join (art. 43/44 AIG).
- The canton decides at its discretion. **SEM approval is mandatory** (art. 2 lit. c ZV-EJPD). [ZV-EJPD art. 2](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2015/518/de#art_2) (v. 2026-09-30)

### 2.4 Non-EU nationals: important public (fiscal) interests (art. 30 para. 1 lit. b AIG; art. 32 VZAE)
- The canton may grant a permit for "significant cantonal fiscal interests" (art. 32 para. 1 lit. c VZAE). Applicants must show they move their **centre of life** to CH and will spend most of their time here. Gainful activity is allowed **only abroad** (art. 32 para. 2 VZAE), apart from managing your own assets. [SEM Weisungen AIG §5.5](https://www.sem.admin.ch/dam/sem/de/data/rechtsgrundlagen/weisungen/auslaender/weisungen-aug-d.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
- These are "may" provisions with wide discretion (BVGer F-4448/2023 of 11 June 2024). Fiscal interest is weighed against Switzerland's reputation. Per the BVGer, admitting wealthy Russian nationals for purely fiscal reasons would likely harm Switzerland's standing (sanctions context). A large donation alone is not a "cultural interest". [SEM Weisungen AIG §5.5](https://www.sem.admin.ch/dam/sem/de/data/rechtsgrundlagen/weisungen/auslaender/weisungen-aug-d.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
- **SEM approval is mandatory** for art. 32 VZAE permits (art. 5 lit. e ZV-EJPD). The canton tells the applicant it intends to approve, then sends the case to SEM, which may refuse, limit or attach conditions (art. 86 VZAE). [ZV-EJPD art. 5](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2015/518/de#art_5); [SEM Weisungen AIG §1.3.1](https://www.sem.admin.ch/dam/sem/de/data/rechtsgrundlagen/weisungen/auslaender/weisungen-aug-d.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
- Published cantonal thresholds:

## 3. Property purchase by foreigners

### 3.1 Lex Koller (BewG, SR 211.412.41; BewV, SR 211.412.411)
- "Persons abroad" (need a permit to buy residential property): EU/EFTA nationals (and UK nationals covered by the Citizens' Rights Agreement) **without** lawful, actual domicile in CH; nationals of other states **without the right to settle** (i.e. no C permit); foreign-seated companies and Swiss companies controlled by persons abroad. [art. 5 BewG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1984/1148_1148_1148/de#art_5) (v. 2026-09-30)
- In practice: **EU/EFTA nationals living in CH (B or C)** and **non-EU nationals with a C permit** living in CH are **not** subject. **Non-EU nationals with a B permit** are subject, but may buy a main residence without a permit (below). [BJ Merkblatt (1 Jul 2009) §5a](https://gni.tg.ch/public/upload/assets/15188/BewG-Merkblatt.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30; leaflet dated 2009, statute text confirmed current)
- **No permit needed** for a **main residence at the buyer's lawful, actual domicile** (natural persons) or for a permanent business establishment. [art. 2 para. 2 BewG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1984/1148_1148_1148/de#art_2) (v. 2026-09-30)
  - Actual domicile must be proven. A residence permit plus commune registration alone is not enough. Indicators: family household, vehicle registration, full tax liability, clubs. [BJ Merkblatt §5a](https://gni.tg.ch/public/upload/assets/15188/BewG-Merkblatt.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
- **Holiday homes:** annual national cap **max. 1,500 quota units**, split into cantonal quotas by the Federal Council. Cantons allocate them and may add restrictions. Unused units carry over one year. [art. 11 BewG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1984/1148_1148_1148/de#art_11); [art. 9 BewV](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1984/1164_1164_1164/de#art_9) (v. 2026-09-30)
  - Per-canton quota figures (Annex 1 BewV) — **UNVERIFIED** (not extracted).
  - Holiday homes are only possible where cantonal law provides it (tourist places) — [BJ Merkblatt §2](https://gni.tg.ch/public/upload/assets/15188/BewG-Merkblatt.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
- **Size limits (holiday/second homes):** net living area normally **≤ 200 m²**; land ≤ **1,000 m²** (non-condo). [art. 10 BewV](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1984/1164_1164_1164/de#art_10) (v. 2026-09-30)
- Conditions: only one holiday/second home per family (buyer, spouse, children under 18); a holiday home **may not be rented out year-round**; a second home must be sold within 2 years once no longer used as such. [art. 11 BewV](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1984/1164_1164_1164/de#art_11) (v. 2026-09-30)
- **Reform pending (2026):** the Federal Council opened a consultation on **15 Apr 2026** (until 15 Jul 2026). Proposals: **third-country nationals would need a permit even to buy a main residence** and must sell within 2 years if they move away; tighter holiday-home quotas; foreign-to-foreign holiday-home resales would count toward quotas; no buying of rental commercial property; a ban on buying listed residential property company shares and real-estate fund units. [admin.ch press release 15 Apr 2026](https://www.admin.ch/de/newnsb/4mzivVX5Ko4dpap06YtuI); [BJ](https://www.bj.admin.ch/de/grundstueckerwerb-durch-personen-im-ausland) (v. 2026-09-30). **Not law yet.**

### 3.2 Second-home law (Lex Weber, ZWG)
- In communes with a **second-home share above 20%**, no new second homes may be approved. The same applies if a permit would push a commune over 20%. Exceptions are in art. 7–9, 26, 27 ZWG. [art. 6 ZWG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2015/886/de#art_6) (v. 2026-09-30)

## 5. Other taxes and origin-country exit rules

### 5.1 Swiss wealth, capital gains, withholding, inheritance
Main source: [ESTV "Geltende Steuern" (Stand der Gesetzgebung 1 Jan 2026, June 2026 edition)](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/c-geltende-steuern-de.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
- **Wealth tax**: cantonal/communal only (no federal wealth tax), on net worldwide wealth (excluding foreign real estate/PEs by allocation rules; this last point is **UNVERIFIED** here). Mostly progressive. **Flat rates in LU, UR, SZ, OW, NW, GL, AI, SG, TG**. The 2025 threshold where wealth tax starts (married, no children) ranged from **CHF 51,000 to CHF 410,000** by canton. For lump-sum taxpayers, cantons decide how wealth tax is covered (see §1.7).
- **Capital gains on private movable assets** (securities, art, etc.) are **tax-free** at federal and cantonal level. Real-estate gains are taxed by a separate cantonal tax.
- **Withholding tax (Verrechnungssteuer)**: **35%** on Swiss investment income (dividends, bond/bank interest) and certain lottery winnings; 15% on life annuities/pensions; 8% on other insurance benefits. Refundable to Swiss residents who declare the income.
- **Inheritance/gift tax** (cantonal only; no federal):
  - **Surviving spouse / registered partner exempt in all cantons.**
  - **Direct descendants exempt in most cantons**, except **AI** (CHF 300,000 deduction), **VD** (CHF 1 m exempt, then degressive deduction up to CHF 2 m), **NE** (CHF 50,000 deduction). In **LU** only communes may tax descendants (exemption limit CHF 100,000).
  - **SZ and OW levy neither inheritance nor gift tax.** **LU** levies no gift tax (gifts within 5 years of death are added back).
  - **GE:** the spouse/descendant exemption **does not apply** if the deceased/donor was **lump-sum taxed** in one of the last three final assessments before death/gift.
  - VS also exempts cohabiting partners (5+ years or joint children).
  - Movables are taxed in the canton of last domicile; real estate where it is located.
  - [ESTV Geltende Steuern §3.1.6](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/c-geltende-steuern-de.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)

### 5.2 UK (from 6 April 2025)
- **Remittance basis abolished.** 2024-25 was the last year it could be claimed. [HMRC technical note](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/changes-to-the-taxation-of-non-uk-domiciled-individuals/technical-note-changes-to-the-taxation-of-non-uk-domiciled-individuals) (v. 2026-09-30)
- **4-year FIG regime:** new residents after **10 consecutive tax years of non-residence** can opt, year by year, to exempt foreign income and gains for up to 4 tax years. Claiming costs the personal allowance and the CGT annual exempt amount. [HMRC HS266 (2026)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/foreign-income-and-gains-fig-regime-self-assessment-helpsheet-hs266/hs266-foreign-income-and-gains-fig-regime-2026) (v. 2026-09-30)

[HMRC RDRM73400](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/residence-domicile-and-remittance-basis/rdrm73400) (verified from gov.uk search excerpt, v. 2026-09-30)

## 6. Tax rulings (Steuerruling / Vorbescheid)
- A ruling is a **written, advance assessment** by the cantonal tax authority of a **concrete** set of facts that is actually about to happen. Hypothetical planning questions are not answered (ZH practice). [ZH Steuerbuch ZStB 106.2](https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/treuhaender/steuerbuch/steuerbuch-definition/zstb-106-2.html) (v. 2026-09-30)
- It binds the authority under **good faith** (art. 5 para. 3 / art. 9 BV) if: the answer was given without reservation to a specific person on concrete facts; the authority was competent; the taxpayer could not see it was wrong; the taxpayer acted on it in ways that cannot be undone without harm; and the law has not changed since. Usually free (fees possible for extensive requests). [ZH ZStB 106.2](https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/treuhaender/steuerbuch/steuerbuch-definition/zstb-106-2.html) (v. 2026-09-30)
- **Relocation use:** the lump-sum amount is agreed with the canton **before or on arrival**. In GE the applicant proposes an amount with a lifestyle form. In VD the **signed lump-sum agreement is a required document for the non-EU residence permit**. [ge.ch](https://www.ge.ch/imposition-apres-depense/comment-beneficier-imposition-apres-depense); [vd.ch](https://www.vd.ch/population/population-etrangere/entree-et-sejour/etats-tiers/sejour-en-raison-dinterets-publics-majeurs) (v. 2026-09-30)
- ZH requires a tax-authority confirmation of the expected ~CHF 1 m tax for the fiscal-interest permit. [ZH Weisung §8.1](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/migration-integration/einreise-aufenthalt/weisungen/Erwerbslose%20Wohnsitznahme.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)

### B.1 Lump-sum taxation

Only Thurgau sets a minimum *tax* (CHF 150,000 cantonal+communal, §1.7). [art. 14 DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de#art_14)

- **Conditions: not Swiss; unlimited liability for the first time or after ≥10 years' absence; no gainful activity in CH** — VERIFIED (art. 14 paras. 1–2 DBG; KS 44 §2). Both spouses must qualify.

### B.2 Investment, "golden visa", tax deal vs permit
- **No golden visa / no statutory CHF 250k or CHF 1m investor route** — VERIFIED. Neither the AIG nor the VZAE contains an admission ground based on an investment amount; the admission grounds for non-EU nationals without work are art. 27–30 AIG (education, retirement, medical treatment, derogations incl. art. 30 para. 1 lit. b). "CHF 250k" / "CHF 1m" figures circulating online are not in federal law. [AIG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2007/758/de)
- **A lump-sum tax agreement ≠ a residence permit** — VERIFIED. Art. 30 para. 1 lit. b AIG + art. 32 VZAE are "may" provisions (wide discretion, BVGer F-4448/2023); cantonal approval is followed by **mandatory SEM approval** (art. 5 lit. e ZV-EJPD). See §2.4.
- **Third-country self-employed route** — VERIFIED. Art. 19 AIG: admission "may" be granted if (a) in the **overall economic interest** (gesamtwirtschaftliches Interesse), (b) financial and operational requirements are met, (c) a sufficient independent livelihood exists, (d) quota (art. 20) and personal requirements (art. 23–25) are met. SEM expects **lasting positive effects on the Swiss labour market** (diversification of the regional economy, jobs for local workers, substantial investment, new orders), a **business plan** (activities, financing, market, staffing, investments, turnover), and company-formation documents; first permits are limited to **max. 2 years**, renewal depends on reaching the business-plan targets (art. 62 lit. d AIG). **No statutory minimum investment or job number.** Cantonal preliminary decisions under art. 19 AIG need **SEM approval** (art. 1 lit. a ch. 1 ZV-EJPD). [art. 19 AIG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2007/758/de#art_19); [W-AIG-4 §4.7.2.1–4.7.2.3](https://www.sem.admin.ch/dam/sem/de/data/rechtsgrundlagen/weisungen/auslaender/weisungen-aug-kap4-d.pdf.download.pdf/weisungen-aig-kap4-d.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
- **Incorporating a company gives no right of residence** — VERIFIED. A company formation is examined under art. 19 AIG like any self-employment (SEM checks whether the founder is self-employed or employed); the Handelsregister entry is only a supporting document. [W-AIG-4 §4.7.2.1, §4.7.2.3](https://www.sem.admin.ch/dam/sem/de/data/rechtsgrundlagen/weisungen/auslaender/weisungen-aug-kap4-d.pdf.download.pdf/weisungen-aig-kap4-d.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)

### B.4 C permit and naturalisation
- **C after 5 or 10 years depending on nationality** — VERIFIED. Legal right after 5 years (settlement agreements): **LI, BE, DK, DE, FR, GR, IT, NL, AT, PT, ES**. Possible (no right) after 5 years: **AD, FI, IE, IS, LU, MC, NO, SM, SE, VA, UK, Canada, USA** — integration criteria (incl. language) apply. Others: 10 years (art. 34 para. 2 AIG). **Early C after 5 years** of continuous B for good integration and language (art. 34 para. 4 AIG; **B1 oral and A1 written** in the local national language, art. 62 para. 1bis VZAE; [art. 62 VZAE](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2007/759/de#art_62)). [W-AIG §0.2.1.3 and §3.5](https://www.sem.admin.ch/dam/sem/de/data/rechtsgrundlagen/weisungen/auslaender/weisungen-aug-d.pdf.download.pdf/weisungen-aig-d.pdf); [art. 34 AIG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2007/758/de#art_34) (v. 2026-09-30). Note: the "13 EU states" 10-year line in §2.1 concerns EU nationals *without* a settlement agreement; UK/US/CA are at the canton's discretion after 5 years.
- **Ordinary naturalisation** — VERIFIED. **C permit** at application and **10 years' residence in total, 3 of them in the last 5 years** (art. 9 para. 1 BüG). Years **between ages 8 and 18 count double**, but actual residence must be at least **6 years** (art. 9 para. 2 BüG). Counted: B and C permits (and FDFA legitimation cards); **F (provisional admission) counts half**; L and asylum-seeker N are not listed and do not count (art. 33 para. 1 BüG). Leaving for more than 6 months ends residence (art. 33 para. 3). **Cantonal minimum residence 2–5 years** (art. 18 BüG). **Language: B1 oral, A2 written** in a national language (art. 6 BüV). [BüG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2016/404/de); [BüV art. 6](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2016/405/de#art_6) (v. 2026-09-30)
- **Simplified naturalisation for spouses of Swiss citizens** — VERIFIED. **3 years of marital union + 5 years' residence in total, including the year immediately before applying** (art. 21 para. 1 BüG); living abroad: 6 years' marriage + close ties (art. 21 para. 2). [art. 21 BüG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2016/404/de#art_21) (v. 2026-09-30)

### B.5 EU/EFTA
- **Non-active EU/EFTA: sufficient means + health/accident insurance, no investment** — VERIFIED (§2.1; art. 24 Annex I FZA).
- **EU/EFTA B valid 5 years** — VERIFIED (§2.1).
- **Notification procedure ≤ 3 months** — VERIFIED. EU/EFTA employees taking up a job with a Swiss employer, posted workers and self-employed service providers from the EU/EFTA need only an online notification (no permit) for work of up to **3 months or 90 working days per calendar year**. It concerns **work**, not residence without activity. [W-VFP §3.3](https://www.sem.admin.ch/dam/sem/de/data/rechtsgrundlagen/weisungen/fza/weisungen-fza-d.pdf.download.pdf/weisungen-fza-d.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30)
- **Family reunification (FZA)** — VERIFIED. Family members = **spouse; descendants under 21 or dependent; ascendants (parents, grandparents) who are dependent** (art. 3 para. 2 Annex I FZA). Derived right, exists only as long as the main right. For EU/EFTA **students** the circle is narrower (spouse + dependent children). [W-VFP §7.1.2](https://www.sem.admin.ch/dam/sem/de/data/rechtsgrundlagen/weisungen/fza/weisungen-fza-d.pdf.download.pdf/weisungen-fza-d.pdf) (v. 2026-09-30). **Supersedes the UNVERIFIED note in §2.7.**
- **Family reunification (AIG)** — VERIFIED. Must be requested **within 5 years**; **children over 12 within 12 months**; deadlines start when the permit is granted or the family relationship arises; late reunification only for important family reasons (art. 47 AIG). Conditions for B holders: see §2.7 (art. 44 AIG: shared home, suitable housing, no social assistance, language, no supplementary benefits). [art. 47 AIG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2007/758/de#art_47) (v. 2026-09-30)
- **Updated FZA (Switzerland–EU package / "Bilaterals III") not in force** — VERIFIED. Federal Council adopted the dispatch on **13 Mar 2026**; parliamentary debate ongoing; optional referendum planned. Nothing changes for residents until entry into force. [admin.ch – Paket Schweiz-EU](https://www.admin.ch/gov/de/start/dokumentation/paquetsuisseue.html); [EDA – Botschaft](https://www.europa.eda.admin.ch/de/botschaft-paket-schweiz-eu) (v. 2026-09-30)

### B.6 Retirees, tax residence, wealth/inheritance
- **Retirees 55+: special personal ties + sufficient means; not a general retirement visa** — VERIFIED (§2.3; art. 28 AIG, art. 25 VZAE; discretionary, SEM approval).
- **Tax residence: 30 days with / 90 days without gainful activity, not 183** — VERIFIED. Tax *domicile* = residing with intent to stay permanently (art. 3 para. 2 DBG); tax *stay* = at least **30 days with** gainful activity or **90 days without**, ignoring short interruptions (art. 3 para. 3 DBG; same rule art. 3 StHG). The "183-day rule" is a treaty/employment-income concept, not the Swiss domestic test. Where two states claim residence, the **tie-breaker in art. 4 of the applicable DTA** (permanent home, centre of vital interests, habitual abode, nationality) decides. [art. 3 DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de#art_3) (v. 2026-09-30). DTA tie-breaker wording per treaty — not re-checked individually (general OECD-model principle).
- **Wealth tax cantonal only** — VERIFIED (§5.1).
- **Inheritance/gift tax cantonal; none in SZ and OW; spouses exempt everywhere, descendants mostly** — VERIFIED (§5.1; exceptions AI, VD, NE, LU communes; GE lump-sum exception).

### A.1 Entry and visa requirements by nationality
Source: [SEM — Overview of ID and visa provisions according to nationality (Annex CH-1, List 1)](https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/en/home/publiservice/weisungen-kreisschreiben/visa/liste1_staatsangehoerigkeit.html), version of 3 April 2026 ([PDF](https://www.sem.admin.ch/dam/sem/en/data/rechtsgrundlagen/weisungen/visa/bfm/bfm-anh01-liste1-e.pdf.download.pdf/bfm-anh01-liste1-e.pdf)); in case of dispute only the HTML version is valid.
- **Visa required both for stays of up to 90 days and for stays of more than 90 days** (ordinary passports): Algeria, Angola, Cameroon, Côte d'Ivoire, Egypt, Ethiopia, Ghana, Kenya, Morocco, Namibia, Nigeria, Rwanda, Senegal, South Africa, Tanzania, Tunisia, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe. Several of these states have exemptions for diplomatic, service or official passports only (codes "M:"/"F:" in the list).
- **No visa for stays of up to 90 days, visa required for stays of more than 90 days**: Mauritius and Seychelles (code V1: a visa is still needed to work, apart from narrow exceptions).
- Code V: third-country nationals who hold a valid residence permit issued by a Schengen state, or a valid type D visa, together with a recognised travel document are exempt from the visa requirement.
- Note B of the list: short stays in the Schengen area are limited to 90 days in any 180-day period. Note C: for stays in Switzerland of more than 90 days a residence permit is required; in principle it has to be applied for at the cantonal migration office before entering Switzerland, also for nationals who need no visa.
- Travel document for a short stay: recognised, issued within the last 10 years and valid for at least 3 months after the intended departure from the Schengen area (note A of the list).
- Procedure: category D visas are issued for stays exceeding 90 days ("national visa"). The application is made at the Swiss representation abroad responsible for the applicant's place of residence, which forwards it to the cantonal migration office for examination. Swiss representations abroad issue and refuse visas. An application can be submitted 6 months before the intended trip and should ideally be submitted at least 2 months before travelling (SEM FAQ wording for visa applications in general; not used in content because the long-stay timing depends on the cantonal procedure). [SEM — FAQ on entry](https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/en/home/themen/einreise/faq.html); [SEM — Entry with visa](https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/en/home/themen/einreise/info-einreise/voraussetzungen-nach-staat/mit-visum.html) (v. 2026-10-01)

### A.3 Swiss banks: identification and origin of assets
- Financial intermediaries must verify the identity of the customer (art. 3 GwG), establish the beneficial owner (art. 4 GwG) and identify the nature and purpose of the business relationship; the amount of information to be collected depends on the risk the customer represents (art. 6 para. 1 GwG). They must clarify the background and purpose of a transaction or relationship that appears unusual or carries a higher risk (art. 6 para. 2 GwG). Relationships with **foreign politically exposed persons** and persons close to them always count as higher-risk (art. 6 para. 3, art. 2a GwG). [Fedlex — GwG/AMLA, SR 955.0](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1998/892_892_892/de#art_6) (consolidation of 1 March 2024, read via the OpenCaseLaw Fedlex mirror; v. 2026-10-01)
- Practical consequence used in content: expect to document how the wealth was built (sale contracts, audited accounts, tax returns, inheritance papers) before funds are transferred. The list of documents is practice, not a legal list.
